## Twenty minutes

| Time | Activity | Evidence to retain |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Expert chooses one learning objective and describes a good example | Their objective and observable behavior |
| 2–9 | Play continuously; write one optional reason and attach a relevant report | Exercise ID, order and observation ticks; confusion or idle moments |
| 9–13 | End for review; inspect one decision and a changed or derivative report | What was available then, what was learned later, one teaching question |
| 13–17 | Branch before that decision and try an alternative | Parent/fork IDs, reason for the alternative, what actually changed |
| 17–20 | Discuss usefulness and entertainment | One misleading abstraction, one tedious moment, one valuable interaction, top three changes |

Do not add mandatory question stops during play. Let the facilitator take notes. If a game ends early, use the remaining time for its review and branch rather than invent a successful run.
