# Shared practice campaigns in REPLAY0.10.1

An instructor with native sharing permission creates a campaign and issues its invitation from the campaign panel. Commanders and analysts sign in separately and join using that invitation. Sharing permission is a native Kamiwaza capability; an editor can play but cannot issue invitations. Campaign membership carries into each fresh mission. Each mission is a new world; this is a sequence of exercises, not a single uninterrupted world.

The intelligence seat can write a Team assessment during play. Commanders see those assessments with source links. These are participant interpretations, separate from personal coaching. Review shows selected key moments and recent orders with observed/recorded timing. The selection is an aid to review, not a score or proof of causality. Exact-intent grouping still leaves repeated-looking moments when an automated policy varies only the troop count.

Participants can leave; the owner can remove a participant or lift a removal. Lifting does not re-seat someone: that participant must explicitly rejoin. Ending a campaign stops future missions while its current mission can continue as a detached exercise. A Review position stays fixed during automatic progression. The owner controls progression; other participants cannot pause or stop it.

The native qualification used three synthetic sign-ins, two very short missions and an independent branch. It verified live shared assessments, member carryover, frozen review, removal/rejoin and canonical reconstruction, with no additional model calls. This is software integration evidence. It does not establish sustained human pacing, fun, military realism or learning efficacy.
